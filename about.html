Our Identity

A Vision for Zimbabwe

Jovo Tech Investments is a business development and consultancy firm focused on building sustainable, technology-driven, and agriculture-centered enterprises.

We work with farmers, entrepreneurs, students, and innovators to turn ideas into profitable and impactful ventures. Our local expertise, combined with a forward-looking mindset, allows us to bridge the gaps in Zimbabwe's most critical sectors.

Our Vision

To be a transformative force in Zimbabwe’s development by building sustainable enterprises that uplift communities and unlock national potential.

Our Mission

To deliver innovative, inclusive, and high-quality solutions across agriculture, education, and housing sectors through strategic partnerships and community engagement.
