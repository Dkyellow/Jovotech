Overview

The Rural Enterprise Growth & Market Access Programme (REGMAP) is Jovotech Investments’ flagship initiative to transform smallholder farming into commercially viable enterprises. REGMAP integrates inputs, training, finance access, market linkages, and technical support to ensure farmers not only produce but also profit.

Kick-Off Project: Commercial Rabbit Production

Our first project focuses on commercial rabbit production, selected for rapid reproduction cycles, low initial investment, and strong market demand. This provides a practical entry point for farmers to build capacity, income, and enterprise knowledge.

  • Target: 10,000 farmers by Q4 2026.
  • Support: Infrastructure, feed, breeding stock, and technical guidance.
  • Market: Aggregation, processing, and guaranteed market access.

REGMAP Rabbit Value Chain – Investment Brief

Jovotech’s REGMAP rabbit value chain is designed for rapid growth and high returns. Rabbits reach reproductive maturity in 4–5 months, offering quick turnover.

Sustainable Powerhouse

Prickly Pear (Opuntia spp.)

A resilient and versatile succulent plant that thrives in semi-arid and arid climates. Well-adapted to drought and poor soils, it offers remarkable benefits for agriculture, nutrition, industry, and environmental sustainability.

Scaling & Impact

At Jovotech Investments, we are scaling up prickly pear production — targeting 1,000 hectares by 2030, aggregating farmers, and establishing processing capabilities to unlock value across multiple sectors.

  • Food & Nutrition: Fruits are rich in Vitamin C and antioxidants. Pads (Nopales) are high in fiber and calcium.
  • Fodder: High-value dry-season fodder for cattle, goats, and sheep.
  • Health: Supports blood sugar regulation and digestive health.
  • Cosmetics: Seed oil promotes skin hydration and elasticity.
  • Environment: Drought-tolerant, reduces erosion, and sequesters carbon.
